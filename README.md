## Hi there 👋

I study how language models fail: where they drift, where they can be talked
out of their guardrails, and how to measure it. A decade of programming
industrial welding robots taught me to take systems from spec to production.
My psychology degree in Cognitive Science pursuit helps me shape attacks and 
understand Human/AI relational behavior.

## What I work on

- **Red-teaming & adversarial evaluation:** Participation in Gray Swan
  Arenas. Ongoing prompt-injection research classified with the
  Arcanum PIT taxonomy, diagnosing the defense type before choosing a technique.
  
- **Safety-relevant classifiers:** a harm-to-model signal classifier built on a
  109-record, 3-class multi-turn corpus. The TF-IDF + logistic regression
  baseline scored 97% on a stratified held-out split, and an adversarial eval
  on hard cases surfaced three real issues.
  
- **Grounded local pipelines:** YouTube claim verification, where every claim
  must quote evidence that Python confirms exists in the search results.
  Profiling and specification cut runtime by 55%.
  
- **Agents & tools:** a Socratic coding tutor that teaches without leaking
  answers, BREACH (a HackAPrompt tutor), a job-posting evaluation and resume
  agent, an MCP server for Obsidian and many many more to come.
