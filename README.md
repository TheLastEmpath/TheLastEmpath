## Who I am

Honestly I am here because I invent things. I have been building and learning how things work in the world around me approaching everything with an analytical mind since I was quite young. flavored by personal—albeit creative interests at any given time, I build solutions to help better my life and the life of others. From legos and game editing software to coding and programming, my mind is wired to solve puzzles and build processes that maximize efficiency, but never at the expense of the individual involved. I build solutions that allow humans to work smarter and avoid cognitive burnout, bettering processes that are mental, mechanical, or digital for humanity as a whole. 

With AI as an extension of my mind the sky is the limit, the ideas that form constantly in my mind now have a seamless bridge they can use to reach fruition, the only barrier left it seems is time itself. That being said I find it increasingly difficult to ignore how "called" to the AI alignment issue I feel and ultimately that line of work is where I feel most at home. helping usher in a safe human first world around AI and advocating for safe practices is something I feel I was born to do. Tinkering with projects helps me understand AI on a multitude of levels and I find this depth of research imperative to understanding such technologies.

## What I do:

I study how language models fail: where they drift, where they can be talked
out of their guardrails, and how to measure it. A decade of programming
industrial welding robots taught me to take systems from speculative data to production.
My psychology studies (Cognitive Science) help me shape attacks and understand
human–AI relational behavior that shapes output.

## What I work on

- **Red-teaming & adversarial evaluation:** 12 accepted jailbreaks in Gray Swan
  Arena's Hazard Hunt. Ongoing prompt-injection research classified with the
  Arcanum PIT taxonomy, diagnosing the defense type before choosing a technique.

- **Safety-relevant classifiers:** a harm-to-model signal classifier built on a
  109-record, 3-class multi-turn corpus. The TF-IDF + logistic regression
  baseline scored 97% on a stratified held-out split, and an adversarial eval
  on hard cases surfaced three real issues.

- **Grounded local pipelines:** YouTube claim verification, where every claim
  must quote evidence that Python confirms exists in the search results.
  Profiling cut runtime by 55%.

- **Agents & tools:** Clyde (a Socratic coding tutor that teaches without
  leaking answers), BREACH (a HackAPrompt tutor), the Resume Builder Agent, and
  a Claude Code workflow for managing my Obsidian knowledge base. More to come.
